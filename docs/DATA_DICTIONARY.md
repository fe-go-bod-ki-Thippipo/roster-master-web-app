## FTE definitions
`fte_amount` is currently a DOMAIN on numeric(10,4) with VALUE >= 0. `assignment_fte` additionally requires > 0. Proposed: sum all non-cancelled effective assignment fractions for one employee at every affected date <= 1.0000. Approved FTE as-of date = effective position_fte_history; actual FTE as-of date = sum effective assignments; gap = approved - actual. Home-company attribution must use effective company history, NOT current employees.home_company_id for historical dates.

## Revision 3 — integrity, reporting and security design
- Employee identity: continuous company transfer retains UUID/code; rehire after termination creates NEW UUID/code. No duplicate employee-position assignments for overlapping dates, including secondary assignments; total concurrent assignment FTE <=1.0000 across companies.
- Approval: configure approvers by request type and multiple steps; cross-company reporting requires authorized approvers of both affected companies. Ensure workflow/company/type matches, approver authorization, review-round uniqueness, self-approval restrictions, immutable active versions and idempotent apply.
- Company access: position-owning company may view cross-company assignee employee code, name, position and assigned FTE only. Other profile fields require independent authorization; enforce via backend and database RLS/column-safe projections. Cross-company reporting-line links do not grant profile access.
- Reports: see `DECISIONS.md` Metric × company-view contract and fixture. Historical names and company lineage are resolved AS OF D. Home-company HC includes active employees without assignments; unassigned HC is separate. Unallocated FTE = 1.0000 minus sum of effective assigned FTE for each non-cancelled active employee at report date; do not clamp negatives (D32).
- Proposed trigger design: serialize by employee and validate total FTE over every affected interval at transaction end; deferred check must cover INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE including employee_id, date, status and FTE changes. Validate status 'ended' has effective_to. Reporting-line graph changes must serialize and validate cycles across overlapping effective periods.
- Baseline enums/defaults: consult `001_schema.sql` for authoritative current CHECK and DEFAULT expressions until full column-level transcription is added. Do not treat proposed `scheduled`, `applying`, `apply_failed` statuses as already permitted by current SQL.

## Revision 4 — detailed design corrections N-01 through N-15 (PROPOSED, NOT SQL)
**Identity and time (N-05, N-06, N-11).** Inclusive dates. Employee employment [hire_date,termination_date] inclusive; no assignment past last day worked. For one UUID, company history covers every active day exactly once, with no gap/overlap. A transfer closes A on D, opens B on D+1; gap means new UUID/code and NO prior-record link. Assignment counts on day D iff interval covers D and status IN ('active','ended'); ended requires effective_to; cancelled excluded from actual FTE. Reject assignment outside employee employment or position active lifetime; enforce employee-position nonoverlap and concurrent employee total <=1.0000 across all companies, using per-employee SELECT FOR UPDATE lock (mandatory on every assignment writer) and interval validation. Historic ended assignments are counted within their effective periods.

**Organization lifecycle and temporal integrity (N-02, N-03, N-08, N-12, N-14).** Proposed history tables `company_group_history(group_id,group_name,effective_from,effective_to,source_request_id)`, `company_history(company_id,company_group_id,company_name,legal_name,effective_from,effective_to,source_request_id)`; existing four org history tables are as defined above. Each entity exists on D iff its approved active history interval covers D; deactivation closes interval at last valid date. For every child interval the effective parent must exist and cover the *entire* interval: company->group, division->company, section->division, department->division and optional section (same division), position->department. Use deferred temporal integrity checks, including parent updates/deactivation; reject orphan intervals or split child intervals at parent transitions with approved change. Approved FTE history, assignments and reporting edges must be within effective position existence; position owner as-of D follows position_history -> department_history -> division_history -> company_history. Historical names and group membership come from their respective history tables. All current master names, parents, status, home_company_id, target_fte and parent_position_id are NON-authoritative projections; as-of reporting and RLS must query history/views only. An idempotent activation/reconciliation job updates projections for future-dated approved changes on their effective day and catches up after missed runs. Never depend on a user write to activate scheduled history. Department/position codes are editable only before first operational use, then immutable and never recycled; enforce code reservation and log corrections.

**Reporting lines and approvals (N-04, N-09, N-10).** Proposed `position_reporting_lines`: id UUID PK, position_id FK, supervisor_position_id FK, line_type enum PRIMARY/COORDINATION, effective_from DATE, effective_to DATE NULL, approval_request_id UUID NULL FK, migration_batch_id UUID NULL FK, status enum scheduled/active/ended/cancelled, with CHECK exactly one of approval_request_id or migration_batch_id nonnull (XOR). New edges ALWAYS have approval_request_id and an approved workflow even within one company; only imported legacy edges may have migration_batch_id. Root designation is approved, effective-dated per position; root has ZERO PRIMARY edges, non-root has EXACTLY ONE PRIMARY edge while position exists. Multiple coordination edges allowed. Reject self edges and temporal PRIMARY cycles; coordination cycles require explicit architecture review before SQL. Resolve two required company approver scopes `source_company` and `target_company` from request company_id and target_company_id, and at apply independently compare to position owners as-of effective_date; mismatches require reapproval. Require distinct company approval evidence for each affected company even if both scopes resolve to the same human; for same-company changes one company approval according to configured workflow. Cross-company line changes do not grant cross-company profile visibility.

**Concrete approval schema proposal (N-01, N-04).** `change_requests` add current_review_round integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 1 CHECK >0, applied_at timestamptz NULL, status scheduled/applying/apply_failed/applied alongside baseline states; define state/time CHECK: applied iff applied_at present; scheduled is assigned by the application at final approval when effective_date is in the future; do not enforce clock-dependent status via CHECK; failed retry must preserve request and audit. `request_approvals` add workflow_id UUID NOT NULL; composite FK (request_id,workflow_id) -> change_requests(id,workflow_id), composite FK (approval_step_id,workflow_id) -> approval_steps(id,workflow_id); approval review_round must equal current request round at decision time; no duplicate approver per step/round; enforce step role/user, company scope, self-approval policy. Approval steps must have exactly one of approver_role_id / approver_user_id, and workflow cannot become active with zero steps; active versions immutable. Workflow selection is server-side by request type, company and effective date; prevent overlapping active workflows of same type/company over time, including global fallback precedence. Change request target_company_id cannot equal company_id when request type explicitly requires intercompany movement; validate source_request_id entity/request-type compatibility. Application uses idempotency key/unique apply ledger and locks; no master mutation before final approval and effective date. Exact baseline CHECK/DEFAULT catalog and transition graph must be reconciled before writing 002a; do not mistake proposed status values for current SQL.

**Security and audit (N-01).** Immutable append-only audit via database permissions and triggers; no direct writes to sensitive profile fields from cross-company assignee access. Cross-company position-owning users see employee code, name, position, assigned FTE only via field-limited view/API and row-scoped policies. Define PDPA retention, encryption/access policy for existing plaintext profile phone/email/address/birth_date before 003. Reconcile indexes for every FK and all F/A/T/M tests before migrations.

**Diagram note (N-07,N-13,N-15).** Supersede older conceptual placeholder language with concrete history tables; the ER document contains their relationships and request provenance. `DECISIONS.md` Revision 4 overrides old draft text. Refer to `REVIEW_TRACEABILITY.md` for full finding mapping and acceptance gates.

## Revision 5 — implementation-ready design proposals (NOT baseline SQL)
**Future approval and apply (R4-01).** Final approval transaction obtains deterministic locks for affected employees/positions/company scopes, validates approved overlapping intervals including future history, and inserts approved history plus audit and apply ledger record. History rows become queryable for future-as-of reads immediately; current projection remains unchanged until effective date. Activation job is idempotent, uses effective-date predicate and retry ledger; it never creates first-time history for an approved request. Backdated changes must revalidate existing future commitments and reject conflicts rather than silently invalidate them.

**Ownership impact closure (R4-02/R4-09).** A change request has many `request_affected_companies(request_id,company_id,reason,scope_snapshot_at,approval_required)` and `request_approval_step_instances(id,request_id,approval_step_id,company_id,review_round,required_approvals)`; unique request/company/approver/round as applicable, FK to approved request and company, verified authorization and no self approval unless explicitly permitted. Impact calculation traverses descendants, historical position owners, all overlapping reporting edges and scoped user roles; persist affected IDs/snapshot/hash, recompute at final approval before final approval under locks; after approval, activation must not reopen approvals. One atomic apply only after every affected company has authorized approval. On company ownership transfer, existing company-scoped roles on moved division/department are suspended pending explicit reauthorization; no implicit inheritance across companies. Resolve RLS scopes using history at current date and deny when scope/owner mismatches. Cross-company reporting edges created by ownership movement require approval by both owning companies, not only when edge was initially created. The precise multi-company approval step cardinality and reapproval UX require independent review before 002b.

**Provenance (R4-04).** Every approved history row in employee_company_history, employee_assignments, position_fte_history, company_group_history, company_history, division_history, section_history, department_history, position_history and position_reporting_lines carries exactly one of `source_request_id` or `migration_batch_id` (XOR); source request must be approved and compatible with entity/change type. Imported rows require audited migration batch. Do not pretend these columns already exist in 001.

**Employment and codes (R4-06/R4-16).** Proposed `employees.hire_date NOT NULL`, `termination_date IS NULL OR termination_date >= hire_date`; active at D iff hire_date <= D AND (termination_date IS NULL OR D <= termination_date). Status is a current projection consistent with last-day-inclusive termination; cancellation is a separately audited lifecycle status. Gapless company coverage for each UUID during employment. Employee code registry/reservation remains permanent; no hard DELETE on employee, even after termination. Rehire creates separate UUID/code and no identity link.

**Root and organization (R4-08/R4-12).** Add `position_history.is_root boolean NOT NULL` (effective dated and approved). For each effective position interval, root => zero PRIMARY reporting lines; non-root => exactly one PRIMARY; enforce deferred validation at transaction end and ensure creation of non-root position plus edge is atomic. Proposed company group association may remain NULL (baseline nullable): represent optional cardinality in ER; if group becomes mandatory, obtain separate business approval before changing baseline nullability.

**Approval state machine (R4-03/R4-11).** Proposed transitions: draft -> submitted (requester, validate payload and select/pin workflow ONCE); submitted -> in_review (system, use pinned workflow); in_review -> returned (authorized reviewer, comment; invalidate current-round decisions); returned -> submitted (requester, increment review_round, preserve pinned workflow); in_review -> rejected (authorized reviewer, terminal); in_review -> approved (system, all required step/company approvals verified); approved -> scheduled (system, future effective date and atomic history reservation); approved -> applying (system, due immediately); scheduled -> applying (job, effective date reached); applying -> applied (job, projection reconciled and applied_at set); applying -> apply_failed (job, store failure); apply_failed -> applying (authorized retry, idempotent ledger). A rejected request needs a new request to retry. For future-dated requests, history reservation is committed in the final approval transaction before state becomes scheduled. For immediate requests, reservation and projection apply in the same transaction when feasible; otherwise maintain applying state with read model based on history. Reject/return invalidates decisions for that review round. Each step passes only when distinct authorized approvals >= required_approvals and no active reject; required company approvals are per-company instantiated workflow steps in request_approvals. Do NOT put now()/current_date-dependent predicates in CHECK. Static CHECK may enforce applied_at nonnull for applied status; transition guards belong in transactional service/trigger. Existing baseline status and scope enum require migration mapping before SQL: proposed approval_scope = company, central, affected_company; legacy related_company is excluded from the new 002a enum (verify no production data exists before migration) (do not silently reinterpret).

**002a concrete proposal (R4-10/R4-14).** `row_version bigint NOT NULL DEFAULT 1` plus update trigger on employees, employee_company_history, employee_assignments, positions, position_fte_history, change_requests, request_approvals, approval_workflows, approval_steps and user_role_assignments; extend to organization/history entities in 002b. Proposed `request_apply_ledger(id uuid PK, request_id uuid FK, operation_key text UNIQUE, phase enum history_commit/projection_activation, effective_date date, applied_at timestamptz NULL, last_error text NULL, attempt_count int DEFAULT 0)` with UNIQUE(request_id,phase); serialize writers using employee row lock, then interval-wide FTE check at commit. Proposed entity_type allowlist: employee, employee_company_history, employee_assignments, employee_profiles, user_role_assignments, position, position_fte_history, division, section, department, company, company_group, position_reporting_lines; validate request payload and audit entity type against actual table catalog before SQL. `change_requests.approved_at timestamptz NULL`; review_round and approved_at recorded once final approvals are complete. Baseline enum/CHECK/index catalog remains to be mechanically reconciled in 002a PR.

**Unallocated FTE (D32).** Per active employee as-of D, capacity 1.0000 less effective active/ended noncancelled assignment FTE; no assignments => 1.0000. Sum residual by home company; cross-company position ownership does not change employee capacity.

## Revision 6 — canonical contracts (proposals; replace conflicting draft text above)
**Employee lifecycle (D33–D36).** Proposed status `active|terminated|cancelled`. Cancelled records are excluded from HC for all dates; cannot have any effective non-cancelled assignments, and corrections to approved history require a new approved compensating request. Employee code immutable from INSERT; UNIQUE(employee_code) and database DENY UPDATE employee_code / DELETE employees (including privileged application writers) preserve all-time reservation. For legacy former employees import full records or a permanent code-reservation ledger with auditable source and collision check; never reuse their codes. A rehire always gets new UUID/code with no identity link.
**Request state machine, authoritative enum and baseline mapping (R5-02/R5-03).** Proposed canonical request status: `draft|submitted|in_review|returned|rejected|cancelled|approved|scheduled|applying|apply_failed|applied`. Baseline `pending_review` maps to `submitted`; `pending_approval` maps to `in_review`; retain other baseline statuses only after checking 001 schema catalog and mapping every enum value. `draft|submitted|in_review|returned -> cancelled` only before final approval; `approved|scheduled|applying|apply_failed|applied` cannot directly cancel. Approved future change withdrawal uses NEW compensating request, approved by all affected companies, committing superseding future history atomically while preserving original provenance and audit. Original request remains applied/scheduled as historical evidence, with superseded linkage; activation must resolve the approved effective timeline, not replay superseded projections. `change_requests.workflow_id` nullable in draft, required at first submit and immutable thereafter, including return/resubmit; composite FK approval evidence preserved. Draft CHECK must be `status <> 'draft' OR workflow_id IS NULL` plus `status = 'draft' OR workflow_id IS NOT NULL` only if draft workflow is always NULL. Workflow selected by server once on first submit; resubmit uses pinned immutable version. Baseline enum and FK changes are migration proposals, not present SQL.
**One multi-company approval mechanism (R5-04).** `request_affected_companies(request_id,company_id,reason,impact_hash)` stores the full locked impact set. Instantiate a per-company `request_approval_step_instances(id,request_id,approval_step_id,company_id,review_round,required_approvals)` for each affected company; `request_approvals` references step instance and stores `company_id` through that instance. Do NOT create `request_company_approvals`. A company-authorized approver is a user with a valid role grant for the relevant company at decision time and permission for that request type, checked server-side; central approvers may be extra configured steps but do not replace company evidence. Lock/recompute affected companies BEFORE final approval; changes restart review round. At activation only reconcile approved history, never recalculate scope or seek fresh approval. A later conflicting request must be rejected at its own final approval.
**Role ownership (R5-05).** Proposed `user_role_assignments.granted_owner_company_id uuid NULL FK companies` captured at grant for division/department scopes (retain company_id NULL under baseline scope CHECK), and status `active|inactive|suspended`. Authorization requires the effective owner from history to equal snapshot, else DENY immediately regardless of activation job; explicit reauthorization required after transfer. For future ownership transfer, current-date RLS uses effective-dated history, not stale projection. Define timestamptz-to-local-date conversion for valid_from/valid_to before 003.
**Apply ledger (R5-07).** Only `request_apply_ledger` stores attempts/errors, no duplicate mutable counters on change_requests. `phase=history_commit|projection_activation`; unique operation_key = request UUID + phase + effective_date (and version if a separately approved correction is created). API Idempotency-Key is distinct, scoped by actor/endpoint/payload hash; it does not replace internal operation_key. `approved_at` records final approval; `applied_at` records projection activation. Historical reads use committed history even if projection activation fails.
